## ✨ Apps

### [Will Be Done](https://will-be-done.app/)
Offline-first, self-hosted task planner built around a visual weekly timeline. It keeps a full database in the browser, so every action applies instantly and works offline, then syncs across devices through a server you can run with Docker on SQLite. Recent releases added mindmaps and notes (v0.14) and read-only CalDAV/iCal calendar events shown next to your tasks (v0.15). _Using [HyperDB](https://hyperdb.will-be-done.app/) as its local-first data layer._
